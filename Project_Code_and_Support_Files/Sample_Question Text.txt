Example 4.7. Draw the shear force and bending moment diagrams for the beam shown in Fig. 4.24(a). Clearly mark the position of the maximum bending moment and determine its magnitude.

Beam description (Fig. 4.24(a)):

- Simply supported beam AB of total length 6 m.
- Support reactions: RA = 3.5 kN (upward) at A, RB = 5.5 kN (upward) at B.
- Point loads and UDL:
  - Concentrated load of 1 kN (downward) at point C.
  - Uniformly distributed load (UDL) of 2 kN/m acting downward from D to E (shown as a wavy/coil-like load).
  - Concentrated load of 4 kN (downward) at point F.
- Segment lengths (left to right):
  - A to C = 1 m
  - C to D = 1 m
  - D to E = 2 m
  - E to F = 1 m
  - F to B = 1 m
